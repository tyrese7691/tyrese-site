CaliSpot: getting started

A guide I wrote for new CaliSpot users, by Tyrese Bewry

Find a spot, train, and meet people to train with.

Most of the questions I get from new users are the same handful. This guide answers them in one place, so people can get going on their own and I can spend more time on the problems that need a person.

Your first 5 minutes

  1. Download and sign up. CaliSpot is free on iPhone and Android. Your account works on both, so you can switch phones without losing anything.
  2. Allow location. CaliSpot uses your location to show the spots nearest to you. You can still browse the map without it.
  3. Find a spot. Open the map, or search for a park or area. Tap a spot to see photos, equipment and reviews from other users before you travel.
  4. Check in when you arrive. Checking in starts your session timer and lets others see the spot is active. When you finish, your session is saved to your profile.
  5. Find people to train with. Browse events for regular meetups at parks near you, join a crew, or add friends by scanning their QR code.

Common questions

Is CaliSpot free?

Yes. Finding spots, checking in, logging sessions, events and crews are all free. CaliSpot Pro is an optional subscription with extras like an AI workout generator, and it comes with a 7-day free trial.

I can't see any spots near me. What can I do?

First, check location is turned on for CaliSpot in your phone's settings. Coverage is strongest in London and the UK, and it grows because users add spots. If your local park is missing, add it yourself and it'll appear for everyone once it's checked.

How do I add a new spot?

Use the option to submit a spot, add its location, a photo and the equipment there. Good photos help other people decide whether it's worth the trip.

What are XP and leagues?

You earn XP when you check in and train. Your monthly XP puts you in a league with other users, so there's a bit of friendly competition to keep you consistent.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check notifications are allowed for CaliSpot in your phone's settings. If you've just reinstalled the app or switched phones, open CaliSpot once so it can reconnect. If they still don't arrive, email me and I'll look into it.

Can I use the same account on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Log in with the same email or Google account and your spots, sessions, friends and XP come with you.

How do I delete my account?

Go to calispot.xyz/delete-account and follow the steps. Your data is removed as described in the privacy policy.

Why I wrote this

I handle every CaliSpot support message myself, across in-app feedback, email, Instagram and app store reviews. When I grouped those messages, the same few questions kept coming up: why spots weren't showing, how to add a park, and notifications that stopped after a reinstall.

Answering each one individually doesn't scale. A short guide lets people help themselves straight away, and frees me up for the problems that genuinely need a person. Looking back, it's the thing I'd have written first.

Still stuck?

Email support@calispot.xyz with what you were trying to do and, if you can, a screenshot. I reply to every message myself.
